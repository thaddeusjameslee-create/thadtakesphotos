Where I shoot

I'm based in Jefferson County, Colorado, and travel across the county for sessions. Most happen at South Valley Park, where the hogback ridges and open meadow give a lot of variety within a short walk. Littleton and the wider Denver foothills are easy to reach too — if you have somewhere that means something to you, say so and we'll look at it.

How it works

  1. You send an inquiry through the contact form or Instagram.
  2. We pick a date, a time, and a location together.
  3. We shoot — about an hour, no rush.
  4. You get your edited gallery as digital files.

For parents

Scheduling and questions go through email or Instagram message, and I answer in plain language about what a session costs and what you get. Parents are welcome to come along — plenty do, and it helps to have someone holding a jacket and telling their kid to relax. Photographs arrive as digital files you download and keep; there's no print package to buy and nothing to ship. You can print them anywhere you like.
