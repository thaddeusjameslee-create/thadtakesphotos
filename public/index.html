About

Behind the camera

Hey! I've been photographing the world since I was 10 years old, and in those years of experience I've learned a lot about what makes a good photo. I'm currently a sophomore attending D'Evelyn Jr. Sr. High School in Jeffco.

I am a passionate cellist and competitive rock climber, and also a huge fan of banana bread. I'd be honored to take your senior photos!

Questions

Good to know

How much does it cost?

$100 flat.

How long is a session?

About an hour.

How many photos do I get?

20 edited, full-resolution digital photos.

When do we shoot?

Golden hour — sunrise or sunset, when the light is best.

Where do we shoot?

I've got spots I like, or we can use somewhere that means something to you.

What if it rains?

Not a problem. We'll reschedule.

Can I change outfits?

An hour is definitely enough time for a quick outfit change.

Can I bring props, like my instrument?

Of course.

Can my parents come?

Yeah, parents are welcome.

How long do I have to wait?

I'll have them to you within a week of the shoot.

How much editing do you do?

Color, light, and light skin cleanup. No reshaping.

How do I pay?

I take cash, Zelle, or Venmo.

How do I book?

Send me a message and we'll pick a place, date, and time.

Send me a note and I'll get back to you.
